# Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Verify FSP status and authorised product categories.
- [ ] Confirm each listed insurance solution falls within Lehumo’s current authorised categories and insurer arrangements.
- [ ] Confirm phone number and both office addresses.
- [ ] Add approved email address and operating hours.
- [ ] Connect and test quote, contact and claim forms.
- [ ] Add compliance officer, complaints and ombud information.
- [ ] Obtain legal review of privacy, PAIA and terms pages.
- [ ] Replace all testimonial placeholders with authorised client reviews.
- [ ] Test on real Android, iPhone, tablet and desktop devices.
- [ ] Enable HTTPS, security headers, backups and privacy-aware analytics.
